# Product definition

ProgramPulse AI is an AI Delivery Assurance layer that independently examines evidence across project systems, challenges reported project health, identifies governance failures, and recommends leadership intervention.

V1 demonstrates that positioning through four uploaded sources for one project
snapshot: status report, RAID log, milestone plan and quality summary. It supports
six dimensions: Schedule, Scope, Quality, Dependencies, Actions and Governance.

The product separates reported health from independent rule-calculated health,
retains management claims, and makes evidence and intervention recommendations
available for human review. It does not autonomously govern projects or verify
that every source is true. Direct project-system connectors are future work.

See the [case study](case-study.md) for the product argument, the
[architecture](architecture.md) for implementation and the [README](../README.md)
for current capabilities and local setup.
